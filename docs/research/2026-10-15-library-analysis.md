## Consequences for the design

1. Reflowing PDFs helps few books here. Most English prose already has an EPUB twin, technical books break when reflowed, and scans have no text. It stays deferred.
2. Auto-cropped margins, fit to width and continuous vertical scroll help the ~350 technical and Vietnamese PDFs.
3. Scanned PDFs need screen-sized rendering with a bounded cache. The current navigator is too slow and too memory-hungry.
4. Comic PDFs and slides want one page at a time, fitted whole.
5. Large books (300–1 800 pages) make an outline (TOC) and page jump important (M3).
6. Importing 9.76 GB by copying doubles storage. Folder import that reads files in place, prefers the EPUB twin and skips non-book files is worth pulling forward from M6.
